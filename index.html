03

The scientific method

Stupid ideas always need a good scientific base.

1

Present the idea

Preferably one that sounds wrong within three seconds.

2

Think it through

Add realism, technology and more effort than the premise deserves.

3

Find the benefits

There are usually more than expected.

4

Explore the consequences

Observe disadvantages, loopholes and absurd side effects.
